What is the primary goal of Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)?
The goal of EDA is to understand the structure, patterns, and potential problems in a dataset before modeling. It helps you see class balance, relationships between variables, unusual patterns, and possible data quality issues so you can make informed decisions about preprocessing and modeling.

Based on the plots in this lab, what kind of person had the best chance of surviving the Titanic?
The visual patterns typically show that the highest survival rates were among women, children, and passengers in first class. These groups had better access to lifeboats and were prioritized during evacuation.

Why is it important to visualize data instead of just looking at summary statistics? What can a plot show you that a number like “mean” or “count” can’t?
Visualizations reveal shape, spread, clusters, outliers, skew, and relationships that summary statistics hide. A mean or count gives a single number, but a plot shows the full distribution—how values vary, whether groups overlap, and whether patterns exist that would otherwise be invisible.
